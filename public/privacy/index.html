The short version. These apps are built to keep your data on your phone. Your workouts, sets, routines, habits and check-ins are stored locally and are never uploaded. One small summary per training day is sent to our server, so that your training can appear next to your habits. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

What stays on your phone

Both apps keep their records in a database on the device itself. None of the following is uploaded anywhere:

  • Every workout: its exercises, each set, the weight, reps or time, and whether you completed it
  • Your routines and any exercises you create
  • Your bodyweight and height entries
  • Your habits and daily check-ins in Streak
  • Your settings: units, default rest, theme, sound and haptics

Because this data lives only on the device, uninstalling an app deletes it, and it is not restored on a new phone. It is also not included in our backups, because we never receive it.

What leaves your phone

Your account

If you sign in, an account is created for you by Supabase, our hosting and authentication provider. Depending on how you sign in, this stores your email address and an identifier from Apple or Google. We also keep one profile row: your email address, display name, username, avatar URL, which sign-in method you used, and your time zone.

Your daily workout summary

When you finish a workout, Teras calculates a single row for that calendar day and sends only that:

Field What it is
date The calendar day you trained, in your own time zone
volume_kg Total weight moved that day (reps multiplied by weight, added up)
sets How many sets you completed
workouts How many sessions you logged
level An intensity step from 0 to 4, worked out by comparing that day against your own previous 90 days

That is the whole of it. Which exercises you did, how much you lifted on any particular set, and your bodyweight are not part of this summary and never leave the device.

Using the apps as a guest

You can use either app without an account. In guest mode nothing at all is sent to our server: there is no account, no profile and no daily summary.

How Teras and Streak share your data

The two apps use one account and one database. Streak reads the daily summaries that Teras writes, so it can draw your training as a second wave beside your habits. This is your own data moving between your own two apps — it is not shared with anyone else, and it is not a sale or transfer to a third party.

Who can see your data

Every row in our database is protected by row-level security, which is enforced by the database itself rather than by the apps. The rules permit each signed-in person to read and change only the rows belonging to their own account. Other users cannot read your rows.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics, usage tracking or behavioural profiling
  • No crash or error reporting service

How long we keep it

Your account, profile and daily summaries are kept until you delete your account. Deleting the account removes them from the database. Anything held only on your phone is removed when you delete the app or clear its data.
