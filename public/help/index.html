Teras & Streak

Help

Teras logs your training. Streak tracks your habits. They share one account, and your training shows up in Streak as a second wave beside your habits.

Do I need an account?

No. Both apps work fully as a guest. An account is what lets the two apps recognise each other, so your training can appear in Streak. Sign in with Apple, Google, or a six-digit code sent to your email.

Where is my data kept?

On your phone first. When you are signed in, Teras also keeps a copy of your workouts, sets, routines, custom exercises, favourites and weigh-ins on its own server, linked to your account, so they are backed up. A workout still under way stays on the phone until you finish it, and as a guest nothing is copied. Streak's habits and check-ins stay on your phone. Separately, one summary per training day — the date, your total volume, the sets and workouts you completed, and an intensity level from 0 to 4 — is shared with Streak so it can show your training. The privacy policy sets this out in full.

I got a new phone. Where is my history?

Signed in, your Teras workouts are kept with your account on our server. Sign in with the same account on the new phone and keep the app open for a moment while it's online: your workouts, sets, routines and daily summaries come back on their own. Streak's habits stay on the old phone.

How is the intensity level worked out?

By comparing a day against your own previous 90 days, never against anyone else. A day with no completed sets is 0. Completed sets with no weight is 1. Below your usual quarter is 1, below your usual middle is 2, below your upper quarter is 3, and above it is 4. Once a day is recorded its level stays put, so your past does not shift as you get stronger.

Why do bodyweight exercises count weight?

A pull-up moves your body, so it counts as your reps multiplied by your bodyweight plus anything you added. Each workout keeps the bodyweight it started with, so logging a new weight today never rewrites what you lifted last month.
